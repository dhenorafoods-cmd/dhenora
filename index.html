Our Story

A Family Tradition Since 1987

It Started in a Village Kitchen

Dhenora began in a small village in Karnataka, where our grandmother churned fresh curd every morning using a wooden bilona. The aroma of that ghee is a memory our family has carried for three generations.

Today, we still follow the same slow, traditional method — curd is churned by hand, butter is slow-simmered over a wood fire, and the ghee is rested in clay pots. Nothing is rushed. Nothing is added.

Our Promise

Every jar of Dhenora is made from the milk of grass-fed desi cows, tested in accredited labs, and packed within 48 hours of preparation. If it isn't good enough for our own family, it isn't good enough for yours.

Why It Costs More (and Why That's Okay)

A2 bilona ghee takes roughly 30 litres of milk to make one litre of ghee. Real ingredients, real labour, real time. We don't cut corners — and we never will.
