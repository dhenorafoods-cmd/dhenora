Our Story

From tradition to your table

A Journey of Purity

DHENORA was born from a simple belief — that real, traditional A2 Bilona ghee should be available to every Indian family.

The Bilona Method

Unlike commercial ghee made from cream, we follow the ancient Bilona process. Milk is boiled, curdled into yogurt, hand-churned to butter, and slow-simmered into golden ghee.

Our Promise

No preservatives. No chemicals. No shortcuts. Just pure, traditional ghee.
